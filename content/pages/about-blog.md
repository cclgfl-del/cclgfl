The CCLGFL Blog is operated under the aegis of the Centre for Corporate Law, Governance and Financial Laws at National Law University, Delhi (NLUD). The Blog seeks to facilitate informed and critical engagement with contemporary developments in corporate law, corporate governance, financial laws, and allied areas. It aims to provide a platform for rigorous academic analysis of emerging legal issues and developments that have significant implications for businesses, markets, regulators, and other stakeholders.

The Blog primarily covers the following areas of law:

- Corporate Law
- Corporate Governance
- Securities Laws
- Banking and Finance Law
- Insolvency Laws

The above areas are indicative rather than exhaustive, and the Blog remains open to scholarship engaging with emerging or interdisciplinary issues that further the academic objectives and broader mandate of the Centre.
