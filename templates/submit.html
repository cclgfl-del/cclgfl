What we publish

The blog invites submissions from students, academicians and practitioners on contemporary issues in corporate law, corporate governance, securities and financial laws, mergers and acquisitions, insolvency and restructuring, corporate finance, and allied areas that further the objectives of the Centre.

Preparing the manuscript

  • Microsoft Word, .doc or .docx only.
  • Times New Roman, 12 point, 1.5 line spacing, with 6 points before and after each paragraph and one-inch margins.
  • Title in bold and underlined; second-level headings in bold; third-level headings underlined.
  • No borders, graphs or images unless they are essential to the argument.
  • Name the file {{ g.filename_pattern }}.

Citations

Cite by hyperlink wherever a source is available online. Use endnotes only for sources that cannot be linked, set in Times New Roman, 12 point, single-spaced.

Footnotes are not accepted, and endnotes should not carry argument of their own.

On the blog, endnotes appear beside the paragraph they belong to, in the margin.

Anonymity and originality

  • Submissions are reviewed without the author's name. The manuscript and its file properties must not identify you.
  • The post must be your original work, not published or under consideration elsewhere. Cross-publication is not permitted.
  • Every submission is screened for plagiarism and AI-generated content.
  • On publication, literary rights in the post vest in the Centre.

What happens next

  1. Screening. The manuscript is checked against these guidelines — length, title, citation form, and anything that would identify the author.
  2. Review. The Editorial Board reads an anonymised copy.
  3. Decision. You hear back within {{ g.decision_days }} days of receipt.
  4. Editing. Accepted posts are edited with you, and you see the post exactly as it will appear before it goes live.
  5. Publication. The post goes live with its citation, ready to be cited and shared.
